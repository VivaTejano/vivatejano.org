Why We Exist

Every family album holds a piece of history.

Somewhere in a closet, attic, garage, or photo album is a photograph, concert poster, recording, or story that deserves to be preserved. Viva Tejano exists to make sure those memories are not lost to time.

Our first public initiative invites musicians, families, collectors, photographers, promoters, and fans to help preserve the visual and oral history of Tejano music.

Why Now?

The stories are still here. The time to preserve them is now.

The pioneers who built Tejano music are still with us, but time is precious. Every interview, photograph, poster, recording, and memory preserved today helps future generations understand the people who shaped this cultural tradition.

Mission

Preserve. Document. Educate. Inspire.

Preserve

Protect photographs, recordings, posters, programs, and other cultural materials.

Document

Capture names, places, dates, memories, and oral histories while they can still be told.

Educate

Create resources that help students and communities understand Tejano cultural history.

Inspire

Connect today’s artists and audiences to the legacy they carry forward.

History Lives Here

From personal archives to public memory.

Our Vision

Building the future home of Tejano history.

Documentation Day is the first step toward a larger vision: a digital archive, oral history collection, educational programs, community events, and a permanent cultural home for Tejano music.

Our Promise
