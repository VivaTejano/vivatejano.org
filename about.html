About Viva Tejano

Viva Tejano Cultural Arts Foundation was created to document, preserve, and share the stories, photographs, music, and cultural legacy of Tejano music. We begin with Documentation Day and grow toward a digital archive, oral histories, education, and a permanent cultural home.

The work begins now.

As the Foundation grows, this page will expand with additional information, resources, and public updates. The first priority is the Documentation Day initiative and building the foundation for a careful digital archive.
